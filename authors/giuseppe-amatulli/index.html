Dr. Giuseppe Amatulli is a research scientist at Yale University and focuses on large-scale & open-source geocomputation. He has been at the forefront of the Geomorpho90m and Hydrography90m datasets and is actively engaged in teaching the latest advances in geocomputation and machine learning.

Career

  • since 2016 - Yale School of the Environment, Yale University (USA)

  • 2012-2013 - South Dakota State University (USA)
  • 2006-2011 - Institute for Environment and Sustainability (action Forest) of the Joint Research Centre
  • 2005-2006 - Departamento de Geografia y ordenacion del territorio, Zaragoza University (Spain)
